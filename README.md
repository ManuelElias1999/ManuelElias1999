## About me

I'm **Manuel Elias**, a Blockchain Developer from Bolivia 🇧🇴 with 4+ years of experience building Web3 products, smart contracts and decentralized applications.

I focus on turning blockchain infrastructure into products people can actually use.

- 🔗 Building with **Ethereum, Base, Stacks, Stellar, Polygon & Avalanche**
- 💻 Specialized in **Solidity, Smart Contracts, DeFi and Web3 integrations**
- 🤖 Exploring the intersection of **AI Agents + Blockchain**

- 🚀 Founder of **Skylos**, focused on AI and Blockchain products
- 🌎 Active in the Web3 ecosystem and developer communities across LATAM
